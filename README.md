**Purpose**:
The purpose of this application is to help contractors (and others) locate, update, and track devices. 
Personally, my current strategy is to do a walk through of each room within MIB and write down every PCN I can find. 
When the device is deployed, I cross out the PCN to update the list. Enough PCNs crossed out, a new batch of computers comes in, devices are moved ...
All of a sudden I need a new list and I'm walking around the office for 30 minutes. Not only is it a waste of time, but it's tedious.
The goal of this application is to eliminate that. 
**How is it different from CMDB Inventory?**
This application will provide specific information of the whereabouts of specific devices within MIB.
For example, which room the device is located in, whether or not it is boxed up, if it has been newly imaged or customized, etc.
Devices outside of MIB will simply read, "Device is at Fox" for example. Specific information about the exact details of this device are unattainable.
